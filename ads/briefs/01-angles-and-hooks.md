# Angles and hooks

Six angles, ordered by how directly they inherit the landing page's own argument. Each carries
hooks (first 3 seconds / first line), primary text, and headlines in Brazilian Portuguese.

Copy rules that apply to every line below: speak to the *situation*, never to the reader's
face. No scores, no before/after, no guaranteed outcome. See `MASTER_CONTEXT.md`.

## Angle 1 — Invisibilidade (the page's own headline)

> Você entra na reunião, fala, e a conversa segue como se nada. Não é insegurança boba — é uma

> baseado em 10 anos de prática clínica.

> R$47 — menos que uma pizza.

## CTA bank

Use `LEARN_MORE` as the Meta CTA type — the destination is a paid purchase, not a signup.

- `Revelar meu potencial agora`  (matches the page's primary button)
- `Quero minha análise facial`   (matches the page's secondary button)
- `Ver como funciona`
- `Começar por R$47`

Matching the ad CTA to the on-page button text reduces the click-to-page mismatch that shows
up as high bounce on cheap traffic.

## What to avoid in every angle

- Anything that asserts a flaw in the reader ("seu rosto é assimétrico", "você é feio").
- Score, nota, ranking, "de 0 a 10" — contradicts the product's core promise.
- Before/after imagery or phrasing.
- Promising a physical result or a timeline for one. The page promises a *plan* in 12 minutes,
  and says visible adjustments "podem aparecer em semanas" — keep that hedge.
- Reframing the page's testimonials as app results. Jackson S. and Alex M. are describing
  clinical work at Instituto Marcos Gama.
